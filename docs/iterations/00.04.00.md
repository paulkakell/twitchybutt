## Mandatory security gate

`python3 scripts/security_gate.py --evidence build/security-evidence.json --commit "$(git rev-parse HEAD)" --version "$(cat VERSION)"`

Exit 1 blocks release for unresolved Medium/High/Critical or unknown findings; missing/failed/empty scans; wrong commit/version; evidence older than 24 hours; or absent current independent/security-history/deployment-image/M01 review. Accepted and deferred findings do not count as remediation. Closures require a named reviewer and evidence verified against the exact commit. False-positive decisions are reviewable evidence, not suppressions of raw scanner findings.

`--development` checks available scan findings without granting release approval. It explicitly prints release_allowed=false. The separate release-readiness CI job invokes full release mode and must remain red while required reviews are pending. Never add continue-on-error or use the development switch in a release job.
